[ what we build ]

Systems, not tools.

On top of the software you already use. A human watches the output.

  • 01

    Documents that read themselves

    Quote requests, specs and plans arrive by mail and get read, checked and turned into a structured file automatically. Every value traces back to its source. No more retyping.

  • 02

    Systems that finally connect

    Your CRM, mail, Drive and tools stay where they are. We build the layer that links them, so data is entered once and stays correct everywhere. No more copy-paste.

  • 03

    Agents for the work that repeats

    Reports, follow-up and internal updates that look the same every week run on agents that write in your voice and get checked before they go out. No more Monday-morning admin.

  • 04

    Proof before you commit

    Every system runs beside your manual process first. We measure the gap, tune it through a feedback loop, and go live only at an agreed accuracy. No black boxes.

[ frequently asked ]

FAQ.

Where does AI actually add value in my business?

Not only where you lose time. Also where problems stay unsolved because they are too complex for handwork, or where a system simply does it better and faster than a person. We start where the pain is biggest, and build from there.

What kind of business does this work for?

There is no hard cut-off. What matters is that you know where the problems and bottlenecks in your business are today. AI builds on those pain points.

What if AI is not the answer?

Then I will tell you. Plenty of problems turn out to be process problems, and you deserve to hear that before you spend on a build. Sometimes the advice is: fix the structure first, then automate. That is value too.

What exactly is a Company Brain?

The central system AI agents work on. Today your business knowledge sits in heads, inboxes and separate tools. A Company Brain pulls that into one queryable layer, so agents have the context to do real work. We build it step by step, adding direct value along the way.

How do we start?

With the one problem that is costing you the most time or money today. We solve that first, capture the knowledge it needs, and let the next problem build on it.
